PRIVACY POLICY FOR INTERNET CAR RADIO

Last Updated: November 24, 2025

1. INTRODUCTION

Internet Car Radio ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how our application handles information when you use our internet radio streaming service.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Internet Car Radio collects minimal information, stored locally on your device only:

a) Favorite Stations: Up to 16 radio station preferences you save
b) Last Played Station: The most recent station you listened to
c) Fullscreen Preference: Your display preference setting

NO personal information (name, email, phone number, location, etc.) is collected.
NO user accounts are created or required.
NO analytics or tracking data is collected.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

All data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage and is used solely to:
- Remember your favorite radio stations between app sessions
- Auto-resume your last played station when you reopen the app
- Remember your fullscreen display preference

This data NEVER leaves your device and is NOT transmitted to any servers we control.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

The app uses the following third-party services:

a) Radio Browser API (radio-browser.info)
   - Used to browse and search available internet radio stations
   - Subject to their privacy policy: https://www.radio-browser.info/
   - We do not control what data they may collect

b) AllOrigins CORS Proxy (allorigins.win)
   - Used as a technical intermediary to access the Radio Browser API
   - Handles API requests on behalf of the app
   - Subject to their privacy policy

c) Radio Station Streams
   - When you play a station, you connect directly to that station's streaming server
   - Each station may have its own privacy practices
   - We do not control or have access to any data collected by individual stations

5. DATA STORAGE AND SECURITY

All user preferences are stored locally in your device's browser storage (localStorage). This data:
- Remains on your device only
- Is not backed up to any cloud service by us
- Can be cleared by clearing your browser/app data
- Is not accessible to us or any third parties

6. INTERNET PERMISSIONS

The app requires internet access to:
- Fetch lists of available radio stations from Radio Browser API
- Stream audio from selected radio stations
- No other internet activity is performed

7. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. The app is designed to be safe for all ages as it collects no personal information.

8. DATA DELETION

To delete all data stored by Internet Car Radio:
- Clear your browser/app data through your device settings, OR
- Uninstall the application

This will remove all saved favorites, preferences, and settings.

9. CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.

10. YOUR RIGHTS

You have the right to:
- Know what data is stored (listed in Section 2)
- Delete all stored data at any time (see Section 8)
- Stop using the app at any time

11. NO DATA SHARING OR SELLING

We do NOT:
- Share your data with third parties
- Sell your data to anyone
- Use your data for advertising
- Track your usage patterns
- Create user profiles

Internet Car Radio is a simple radio streaming app that:
